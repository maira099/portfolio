Case Study — SaaS Marketing Design

Social Carousels

Concept brand systems, built for the scroll

Client

Self-directed

Business Type

SaaS Marketing

Deliverables

Social Carousel Assets

Year

2026

01 — Overview

Three self-directed concept projects exploring visual identity for SaaS products, built as full Instagram carousel sets rather than single static posts. Each one had to hold a consistent brand system across every slide: same type scale, same colour logic, same voice, whether the slide was a headline, a UI mock, or a photo.

02 — Planova

AI-powered project management

A violet-on-black system for an AI project management tool. Gradient orbs and glass panels carry the "clarity" message across nine slides, from the hook to the feature breakdown to the close.

1 / 9
03 — Vector · Concept A

Build full-stack projects in minutes

A pink-on-black, photographic direction for Vector, an AI build agent. Bold headline slides alternate with product shots and lifestyle photography, tied together with one accent colour and a consistent V mark.

1 / 9
04 — Vector · Concept B

Design powered by AI

A second, quieter direction for the same brand: olive and cream instead of pink and black, 3D render objects instead of photography. Built to test how far the identity could stretch before it stopped feeling like Vector.
