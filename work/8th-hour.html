01 — Overview

8TH HOUR (or Eight Hour / 8TH:HR) is a modern workwear and tailoring brand redefining the corporate uniform. Born out of the tension between office culture and street style, the brand crafts relaxed tailoring for a new generation of creatives.

Tailored Sharp Ironic Modern
02 — Brand Identity

A mark for the hours in between

The identity centres on an hourglass rebuilt as a bowtie — two triangles pinched at the waist, reading equally as a knot or a diamond. It's the visual shorthand for the brand's whole premise: the quiet, tailored transition between the office and everything after it. The wordmark pairs a confident, condensed grotesque with a soft-spoken monogram, 8TH:HR, for smaller applications.

03 — Label Design

Hangtags, buttons & a cufflink that tells the time

Every touchpoint — the woven hangtag, the shirt button, the cufflink — carries the diamond mark at a different scale, so the brand still reads even reduced to a few millimetres of stamped metal.

04 — Imagery Direction

The choreography of getting dressed

The photography direction leans into unposed, in-between moments — hands mid-tailor, a cuff caught in the light, a desk mid-thought — offset by the hourglass as a recurring motif for time spent and time saved.
